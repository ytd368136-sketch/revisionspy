Past Question Paper Solutions:

Q1: How do self-confident children get successful?
Ans: Self-confident children achieve success by bravely embracing new challenges and bouncing back quickly from failures.

Q2: What type of atmosphere should be created at home?
Ans: A positive and supportive atmosphere should be created at home.

Q3: Why is "Pranayama" essential for our body?
Ans: "Pranayama" is essential for our body because it helps in maintaining physical and mental well-being.

Q4: Why does a person fall ill?
Ans: A person falls ill due to a weakened immune system, poor lifestyle choices, or exposure to pathogens.

Q5: What kind of people are remembered in history?
Ans: People who have made significant contributions to society, demonstrated exceptional courage, or achieved remarkable accomplishments are remembered in history.

Q6: Conversation is an art and skill, how?
Ans: Conversation is an art and skill because it requires active listening, empathy, and the ability to express thoughts clearly and respectfully.

Q7: What is the supreme most duty of man?
Ans: The supreme most duty of man is to live a life of integrity, compassion, and responsibility towards oneself and others.

Q8: What do you mean by the word "Self-Welfare"?
Ans: "Self-Welfare" refers to the well-being and personal development of an individual, encompassing physical, mental, and emotional health.
